## 📌 The Problem We Solve &middot; የምንፈታው ችግር
Most private academies, language centers, music schools, and TVET institutes in Addis Ababa juggle **three paper registers** and chaotic Excel spreadsheets:
- **Uncollected Tuition & Drift:** Overdue instalments slip through without timely reminders; cashiers make manual receipt calculation errors.
- **Attendance Loss:** Class registers get misplaced or damaged; trainers cannot record attendance when internet is disconnected.
- **Unverified Paper Certificates:** Forged certificates damage academy reputation and cannot be verified by employers or embassies.
- **SMS Costs:** Sending manual SMS reminders to hundreds of students takes hours and wastes money.

---

## 🎯 The Tsehafi Solution &middot; የጸሐፊ መፍትሔዎች

### 1. Integer Santims Financial Ledger & Gapless Receipts (የሳንቲም ሂሳብና ደረሰኝ)
- Sequential receipt vouchers `#YYYY/0001` resetting each Ethiopian calendar year.
- Amount in words generated automatically in both **Amharic (በአማርኛ)** and English.
- Supports Telebirr, CBE Birr, and Cash with instant cashier cash-up reconciliation.
- Append-only double-entry ledger in integer santims: **0.00% rounding drift**.

### 2. Offline-First Daily Roll Call (ያለ ኢንተርኔት የሚሰራ አቴንዳንስ)
- Trainers mark attendance on their mobile phone or tablet in classrooms.
- **Works 100% offline during telecom blackouts:** Submissions queue locally and sync automatically when internet resumes.

### 3. Verifiable Graduation Certificates (በQR ኮድ የተረጋገጡ ሰርተፍኬቶች)
- Bilingual A4 landscape certificates with custom academy logo, student photo, and course competencies.
- Unique cryptographically secure QR code: **Scan to verify instantly** on any smartphone at `/verify/cert/[token]`.

### 4. Automated SMS Reminders (የኤስኤምኤስ ማሳወቂያዎች)
- Pre-scheduled tuition payment reminders (3 days before due date, on due date, and overdue alerts).
- Student absence alerts sent to guardians.
- Built-in quiet-hours guard (no SMS between 8:00 PM and 7:00 AM).
